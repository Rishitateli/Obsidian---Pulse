OBSIDIAN PULSE

SYSTEM LOGIC |HOW IT WORKS

💚HEART RATE MONITORING (GREEN PPG)

Uses simulated photoplethysmography (530nm green light) to detect volumetric blood flow changes per beat.

ALGORITHM:peak to peak interval detection(60/RR-interval).
🩸BLOOD OXYGEN (SpO2) MONITORING (RED LED)

Stimulates differential absorption of red (660nm)and infrared(940nm) light to measure oxygenated and deoxygenated hemoglobin.

ALGORITHM:ratio of ratios (R/IR) to estimate SpO2.
💧 BLOOD PRESSURE ESTIMATION

Correlates stimulates PPG waveform parameters (pulse transit time & pulse wave velocity) with arterial stiffness

ALGORITHM:linear regression mapping to systolic / diastolic mmHg
👟 STEP COUNTER & SLEEP

Tracks 3-axis accelerometer peaks for gait cycles and combines low heart rate variance with stillness for sleep stages

Formula: distance=steps x 0.75m | calories=steps x 0.04 kcal
